## 🎨 VISUAL IDENTITY

### Overall Aesthetic
**"Cinematic Seerah" — Feature-film quality applied to Islamic history**
- Not "documentary style" — but **cinematic reenactment aesthetic**
- Every frame could be a movie still
- Period authenticity without dusty/museum feel
- Living, breathing 7th century Arabia

### Color Palette
```
PRIMARY (Earth & Faith):
├── Sand/Ochre: #D4A574, #C9966B, #B8865A
├── Terracotta: #C66D4D, #A5553D, #8B4533
├── Deep Brown: #4A3728, #3D2E24, #2C1E15
├── Cream/White: #F5F0E8, #E8E0D8, #DDD5CC (followers' garments)
└── Gold Accent: #D4A843, #C49A3D, #B08835 (Kaaba, prestige)

SECONDARY (Conflict & Power):
├── Dark Red: #8B2E2E, #6B1F1F, #4A1515 (opposition, blood)
```

### Lighting Philosophy
| Scene Type | Lighting Approach |
|---|---|
| **Intimate/Family** | Warm golden hour, single source (fire/lamp), soft shadows |
| **Public/Market** | Harsh midday sun, high contrast, dust motes visible |
| **Torture/Suffering** | Brutal direct sun, no mercy, sweat/shadow detail |
| **Night/Conspiracy** | Firelight only, orange flicker, deep shadows, faces half-lit |
| **Battle** | Cold overcast, flat light, steel glints, breath visible |
| **Spiritual Moments** | Soft divine glow, backlight, ethereal, minimal |
| **Kaaba Scenes** | Golden hour, circumambulation motion blur, sacred geometry |

## 🏛️ ENVIRONMENT DESIGN

### Key Locations (Recurring Assets)
```
MECCA:
├── Kaaba Courtyard — Black stone, gold band, multazam, hatim
├── Abu Talib's House — Modest, clay walls, courtyard, date palms
├── Abu Jahl's House — Grand, columns, fine textiles, guarded
├── Market (Suq) — Narrow alleys, cloth stalls, spice pyramids, noise
├── Safa/Marwa Hills — Rocky, elevated, city view
├── Slave Quarter — Shadows, chains, rough stone, despair
└── Dar al-Nadwa — Circular, elders' seats, serious atmosphere

MEDINA (Later):
├── Prophet's Mosque — Simple, palm trunks, mud bricks, suffah
├── Battlefields — Uhud (mountain), Trench (ditch), Badr (wells)
└── Desert — Infinite, dunes, mirage, stars
```

### Architectural Language
- **Materials**: Mud brick, palm trunks, acacia wood, woven mats, leather
- **Colors**: Unpainted earth, whitewashed only for sacred
- **Details**: Geometric patterns on doors, mashrabiya screens, courtyard centers
- **Scale**: Human-scaled, intimate, not monumental (except Kaaba)

## 👥 CHARACTER DESIGN SYSTEM

### Visual Archetypes (Consistent Across Videos)

#### The Seeker/Protagonist (Ali-type)
- **Young**: Lean, intense eyes, simple wool/linen, hands calloused
- **Adult**: Athletic, beard trimmed, sword at hip, commanding presence
- **Eyes**: Always the focus — determination, then wisdom
- **Color**: White/cream (purity), later green accents (faith)

#### The Wise Elder (Abu Talib-type)
- **Face**: Deep lines, kind eyes, white beard, turban wrapped with care
- **Hands**: Weathered, often holding tea cup or walking stick
- **Color**: Earth tones, quality wool, subtle status markers
- **Lighting**: Always warm, never harsh

#### The Antagonist (Abu Jahl/Abu Lahab-type)
- **Face**: Sharp features, calculating eyes, dyed beard (henna red/black)
- **Attire**: Silk, brocade, jewelry, perfumed, impeccable
- **Posture**: Elevated, seated high, looking down
- **Color**: Dark reds, blacks, gold — wealth as armor
- **Lighting**: Often side-lit, half-shadow, menace

#### The Oppressed (Bilal/Ammar/Sumayyah-type)
- **Body**: Scarred, muscular from labor, chains marks
- **Clothing**: Rough, patched, minimal, dusty
- **Eyes**: Unbroken — the key differentiator
- **Color**: Greys, browns, undyed wool
- **Transformation**: Gradual cleaning/whitening as faith grows

#### The Warrior Convert (Hamza/Umar-type)
- **Build**: Imposing, broad, battle-scarred
- **Transition**: Armor → simple white, sword → staff/spear
- **Face**: Hard lines softening, eyes clearing
- **Color**: Steel/leather → white/green

#### Women (Fatima/Khadija/Asma-type)
- **Modest**: Full hijab/niqab per Islamic guidelines
- **Presence**: Conveyed through hands, voice, posture, eyes only
- **Color**: Deep blues, greens, blacks — dignity
- **Lighting**: Soft, respectful, never objectifying

### Aging System
| Stage | Visual Markers |
|---|---|
| Child (7-12) | Smaller frame, large eyes, simple tunic, barefoot or leather sandals |
| Youth (13-18) | Growing frame, first beard hairs, learning weapons, intense gaze |
| Young Adult (19-30) | Full strength, beard defined, armor/weapons mastered |
| Prime (30-50) | Command presence, gray at temples, scars tell stories |
| Elder (50+) | White beard, slower movement, eyes sharper, teaching posture |

## 🎬 CAMERA LANGUAGE

### Shot Vocabulary
```
ESTABLISHING (10-15s):
├── Wide aerial: City in valley, caravans approaching
├── Kaaba tawaf: Circular motion, crowds, golden light
└── Desert horizon: Infinite, small human figures

MEDIUM/COMPOSITION (5-10s):
├── Two-shot dialogue: Over-shoulder, eye-line match
├── Council circle: Circular framing, power positions
├── Market movement: Tracking through stalls, life details
└── Family meal: Top-down or eye-level, intimacy

CLOSE-UP (3-8s):
├── Eyes: Decision, fear, faith, recognition
├── Hands: Sword grip, chain breaking, Quran holding, tea pouring
├── Lips: Whispered prayer, shouted declaration, "Ahad"
└── Details: Sand in hair, sweat bead, tear, blood drop

EXTREME CLOSE-UP (1-3s):
├── Pupil dilation: Fear → resolve
├── Finger on sword hilt
├── Rosary bead movement
└── Quran page texture
```

### Camera Movement Rules
| Movement | When to Use | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| **Static/Tripod** | Dialogue, councils, emotional beats | N/A |
| **Slow Push-in** | Realization, growing tension, spiritual moment | 10-20s |
| **Slow Pull-out** | Isolation, revelation, "bigger picture" | 15-25s |
| **Tracking (Side)** | Walking through market, following messenger | Match walking pace |
| **Tracking (Forward)** | Approaching confrontation, entering sacred space | Slow, deliberate |
| **Handheld (Subtle)** | Battle chaos, torture, desperate moments | Breath-like |
| **Crane/Drone** | Establishing, battle overview, tawaf circles | Smooth, majestic |
| **NO** | Random movement, shake for "energy", zoom | Never |

### Composition Principles
- **Rule of thirds** for dialogue (eyes on upper third)
- **Center framing** for: Prophet references, Kaaba, moments of decision
- **Negative space** for: Isolation, divine presence, contemplation
- **Leading lines**: Archways, sword blades, gaze direction, Kaaba alignment
- **Depth layers**: Foreground (hands/object), mid (face), background (context)

## 🎙️ NARRATION STYLE (Our Format: Voiced Documentary)

### Voice Character
```
GENDER: Male (traditional authority) OR Female (accessible warmth)
AGE SOUND: 35-50 (mature, not elderly)
TONE: 
  ├── Warm, human, not robotic
  ├── Measured pace — 140-150 words/minute
  ├── Natural Arabic rhythm in English/Arabic
  ├── Emotional range: whisper → intensity → peace
  └── Never: announcer voice, TV narrator, preacher cadence

ACCENT: 
  ├── Moroccan Arabic speaker natural pronunciation
  ├── Classical Arabic terms pronounced correctly
  ├── No forced "religious" affectation
  └── Like a knowledgeable uncle telling family history
```

### Delivery Directives (Mark in Script)
```
[PAUSE]           — 1.5-2s silence, let image breathe
[SLOW]            — Deliberate, weight each word (key facts, names)
[EMOTIONAL]       — Voice cracks, warmth, near tears (sacrifice, mercy)
[WHISPER]         — Intimate, leaning in (secrets, night, prayer)
[INTENSIFY]       — Building power, not volume (confrontation, climax)
[CALM]            — After storm, wisdom, teaching (resolution)
[RHEORICAL]       — Question to audience, pause for thought
[QUOTE]           — Direct speech from sources, distinct tone
```

### Arabic Integration
- **Basmala** at video start: "Bismillah ir-Rahman ir-Rahim"
- **Key terms in Arabic** with first-use translation:
  - Sahabi → Companion
  - Seerah → Biography of Prophet
  - Hijra → Migration
  - Ansar → Helpers
  - Muhajirun → Emigrants
  - Shahada → Testimony of faith
- **Quranic verses**: Recited in Arabic, then translated
- **Hadith**: "The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said..." in measured tone
- **Names**: Full honorifics first time (Ali ibn Abi Talib, radi Allahu anhu)

## 🎵 MUSIC & SOUND DESIGN

### Musical Identity
```
COMPOSER STYLE: Modern cinematic + Traditional Arabic
INSTRUMENTS:
├── Oud (lead melodic) — warm, woody, expressive
├── Ney (flute) — breathy, spiritual, airy
├── Qanun (zither) — shimmering, delicate, heavenly
├── Percussion: Riq, Daf, Tabla — heartbeat, tension, drive
├── Strings (cello/violin) — emotional weight, cinematic scope
├── Male choir (low) — gravity, brotherhood, battle
└── Female voice (wordless) — mercy, divine presence, motherhood

LEITMOTIFS (Create once, reuse):
├── FAITH THEME: Ney + strings, ascending, resolved
├── STRUGGLE THEME: Low cello + slow daf, building
├── VICTORY THEME: Full ensemble, major, triumphal but humble
├── LOSS THEME: Solo oud, minor, space between notes
├── PROPHET THEME: Never melodic — only implied via silence + light
├── ANTAGONIST THEME: Dissonant, low brass, rhythmic threat
└── CHILDHOOD/INNOCENCE: High qanun, simple melody
```

### Sound Design Layers
| Layer | Content | Level |
|---|---|---|
| **Dialogue/Narration** | Primary | 0dB (reference) |
| **Foley** | Footsteps, cloth, chains, sword draw, tea pour, sand | -12 to -18dB |
| **Ambience** | Market, wind, fire, mosque, battle distant | -18 to -24dB |
| **Music** | Score, leitmotifs | -12 to -20dB (duck under narration) |
| **Impacts** | Sword hits, door slam, body fall, heart beat | -6 to -12dB (momentary) |
| **Silence** | Strategic gaps | -∞ (true silence) |

### Silence as Tool
- **Pre-quote**: 1s silence before Quran/hadith
- **Post-emotional**: 2-3s after sacrifice/death scene
- **Realization**: 1.5s when character understands truth
- **Prayer moment**: 3-5s of pure ambient + breath
- **Title cards**: 2s silence before/after

## 📖 EMOTIONAL STORYTELLING PRINCIPLES

### Core Philosophy
> **"History through human choices, not dates"**

### Retention Architecture
```
HOOK (0-30s):
├── Visceral image + universal question
├── "What would you do?" scenario
├── Stakes made personal, not abstract
└── Promise of transformation

CURIOSITY GAPS (Every 2-3 min):
├── Unanswered question planted
├── Character mystery introduced
├── "But what happened to...?"
└── Payoff within 5 minutes

EMOTIONAL ANCHORS (Per Act):
├── Act 1: Innocence threatened (child/slave)
├── Act 2: Loyalty tested (friend/family)
├── Act 3: Conviction vs survival (martyrdom choice)
└── Act 4: Legacy realized (teaching next generation)

UNIVERSAL THEMES (Islamic but accessible):
├── Justice vs Power
├── Truth vs Comfort
├── Individual vs Tribe
├── Faith vs Fear
├── Mercy vs Vengeance
└── Legacy vs Ego
```

### Islamic Sensitivities (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
```
PROPHET MUHAMMAD (ﷺ):
├── NEVER depicted (face, body, voice, shadow)
├── Referenced only: "The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ)", "he (ﷺ)"
├── Actions described by witnesses: "They saw him (ﷺ)..."
├── Words: Only authenticated hadith, marked [QUOTE]
└── Presence felt through: Light, others' reactions, environment

OTHER PROPHETS:
├── Same rule — no depiction
├── Stories told through: Landscape, objects, followers' eyes
└── Quranic narrative style: "And We sent..." passive, divine

COMPANIONS (Sahaba):
├── Depicted with dignity, historical accuracy
├── Physical descriptions from authentic sources only
├── No invented dialogue presented as historical fact
├── Uncertain details: "It is reported..." or omitted
└── Faces: AI-generated consistent characters, respectful

SACRED OBJECTS:
├── Kaaba: Accurate, respectful, no creative liberty
├── Quran: Never animated opening, pages turning respectfully
├── Prayer: Accurate positions, no music during recitation
└── Mosques: Historical architecture, not modern
```

## 🛠️ PRODUCTION WORKFLOW (Tool-Agnostic)

### Phase Structure
```
1. RESEARCH (Week 1)
   ├── Primary sources: Quran, Sahih Hadith, Seerah (Ibn Hisham, Ibn Ishaq, Al-Tabari)
   ├── Secondary: Academic, verified scholars
   ├── Fact-check: Names, dates, locations, chain of narration
   └── Output: Research document with citations

2. STYLE BIBLE APPLICATION (Day 1)
   ├── Select color palette variant for this story
   ├── Cast character archetypes
   ├── Define location set
   └── Choose leitmotifs

3. SCRIPT (Week 1-2)
   ├── Outline: 8-12 story beats
   ├── Draft narration: 1500-1800 words (10 min @ 150 wpm)
   ├── Mark delivery directives
   ├── Review: Accuracy + emotional arc + retention
   └── Approve before visual work

4. STORYBOARD (Week 2)
   ├── 40-60 scenes for 10 min
   ├── Each: Visual desc + Image prompt + Animation prompt
   ├── Continuity check: Characters, locations, lighting
   └── Approve before generation

5. ASSET GENERATION (Week 2-3)
   ├── Images: Midjourney/DALL-E/Stable Diffusion (consistent seeds)
   ├── Animation: Runway/Gen-2/Pika/Kling (5-10s clips)
   ├── Audio: ElevenLabs/XTTS (narration), custom score
   └── QC: Visual consistency, Islamic compliance

6. EDIT (Week 3)
   ├── Rough cut: Narration + placeholder images
   ├── Fine cut: Replace with generated assets
   ├── Sound mix: Music ducking, foley, ambience
   ├── Color grade: Apply palette LUT
   └── Export: 4K + 1080p vertical/horizontal

7. RELEASE PACKAGE
   ├── Final video (4K 16:9, 1080p 9:16)
   ├── Thumbnails (3 variants)
   ├── Shorts/Reels (3 x 60s clips)
   ├── Description with sources
   └── Subtitles (Arabic, English, French)
```

### Quality Gates
- [ ] **Accuracy Gate**: Every claim sourced before visual work
- [ ] **Sensitivity Gate**: No prophet depiction, respectful language
- [ ] **Consistency Gate**: Characters/locations match across scenes
- [ ] **Retention Gate**: Hook, 3 curiosity gaps, emotional peaks mapped
- [ ] **Technical Gate**: 4K render, proper audio levels, subtitles synced

## 📐 TECHNICAL SPECS

### Final Deliverables
| Format | Resolution | Codec | Bitrate | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Master** | 3840×2160 (4K) | ProRes 422 HQ / H.265 | 150-200 Mbps | Archive |
| **YouTube** | 3840×2160 (4K) | H.264 High | 35-45 Mbps | Primary |
| **Shorts/Reels** | 1080×1920 (9:16) | H.264 High | 15-20 Mbps | Vertical |
| **Backup** | 1920×1080 (1080p) | H.264 High | 10-15 Mbps | Fallback |

### Audio Specs
- **Sample Rate**: 48 kHz
- **Bit Depth**: 24-bit (master), 16-bit (delivery)
- **Loudness**: -14 LUFS (YouTube), -16 LUFS (podcast)
- **True Peak**: -1 dBTP max
- **Channels**: Stereo (narration center, music L/R)

### Subtitle Specs
- **Format**: SRT + ASS (styled)
- **Fonts**: Arabic - Amiri/Scheherazade; Latin - Inter/Source Sans
- **Size**: 8% of video height
- **Position**: Bottom third, safe margins
- **Languages**: Arabic (primary), English, French
